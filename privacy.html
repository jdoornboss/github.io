Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The HushLoop Uploader uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos, thumbnails and video details to the HushLoop channel owned by the operator of this tool.

  • The tool is used only by the channel owner. It does not collect, store or share data about other people.
  • The only Google data it uses is the owner's own sign-in token, which is kept as an encrypted secret and used only to upload to the HushLoop channel.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Questions: contact us via the HushLoop YouTube channel.
